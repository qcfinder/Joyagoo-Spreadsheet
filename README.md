# Joyagoo Spreadsheet

[Read the complete guide](https://qcfinder.github.io/Joyagoo-Spreadsheet/) · [Browse FashionReps Joyagoo product links](https://fashion-reps.net/joyagoo-spreadsheet)

An independent practical guide from product discovery to a ready parcel.

## Included

- Original-source and exact-variant checklist.
- Warehouse QC review and measurement prompts.
- Consolidation and shipping-quote preparation.
- [Blank CSV parcel checklist](parcel-checklist.csv) for Excel and Google Sheets.

## Use the checklist

1. Save original URLs, seller details, variants and dates.
2. Keep order references and QC evidence beside each product.
3. Resolve open questions and record keep or return decisions.
4. Assign approved items to a parcel group.
5. Record packed dimensions, weight, currency, route restrictions and the current quote before dispatch.

## Product discovery

The [FashionReps Joyagoo catalogue](https://fashion-reps.net/joyagoo-spreadsheet) is a starting point. Check original listings and availability. Shared photos and reviews do not guarantee the quality of your own item.
