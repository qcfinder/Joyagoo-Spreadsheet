01 / Build a traceable shortlist

What is a Joyagoo spreadsheet?

A Joyagoo spreadsheet is a working list of product links, variants and buying notes. Use it to compare options and track what still needs checking. The best row explains which item you mean and what evidence supports your decision.

Discover a candidate

Start with the FashionReps Joyagoo catalogue. Save the discovery link and original seller URL separately so you can return to the source.

Match the exact option

Check seller, colour, size, quantity and included accessories. Similar photos or titles do not prove that listings offer the same goods.

Keep a dated record

Record the price, currency and date checked. If a listing disappears or changes, mark it for review rather than silently replacing it.

02 / Turn photos into a decision

Review your own warehouse item.

Keep order references and QC-photo references beside each product row. Shared reviews can suggest questions to ask, but the evidence for your own item should drive your keep or return decision.

03 / Prepare a consolidation checklist

A parcel is only as ready as its unresolved item.

Before combining orders, identify which items are approved and which need more information. Avoid losing individual order references when grouping items into a shipment.

  1. Give each approved item a parcel-group reference.
  2. List packaging requests and check whether they affect protection or dimensions.
  3. Obtain the packed weight, dimensions and a current available-route quote.
  4. Check destination restrictions and the route's tracking and compensation conditions.
  5. Save the final dispatch decision with its date.

Budget for the delivered parcel

Record item amounts, domestic delivery, agent or payment fees, packing, international freight and destination charges separately. Label currencies and distinguish estimates from paid amounts. A larger package may be billed by volume under the route's own rules.

No spreadsheet can promise a fixed shipping price or delivery date. Replace assumptions with the current quote for your actual parcel.

04 / Free CSV template

One row per variant. One clear next action.

The downloadable checklist contains blank headings for original links, order references, QC decisions and parcel groups. Import it into Excel or Google Sheets and add your own records. There are no invented products or endorsements.

Worked example / Illustrative only

From an uncertain row to a dispatch decision.

Scenario: a hoodie with an unclear size chart

Save the original listing and selected colour/size. Suppose your comparison garment has a 58 cm flat chest width, while the listing says “116 cm chest.” First ask whether that number describes body circumference or garment circumference. Do not assume the two measurements are equivalent.

  1. Open question: measurement method is unclear.
  2. Next action: request a flat chest measurement and a photo showing the tape placement.
  3. Evidence: save the response and measurement-photo reference in the CSV.
  4. Decision: compare the confirmed dimensions with your own garment and preferred fit; keep or return under the current terms.

This is an invented teaching scenario, not a test purchase or product recommendation.

Compare two parcel quotes fairly

If route A quotes 25 and route B quotes 22 in the same currency, check whether each includes the same packing, handling and destination charges. Record the quote date and restrictions. Do not select B solely because its headline amount is lower.

05 / Before dispatch

Common questions.

Is this an official Joyagoo spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent planning guide. Refer to Joyagoo for current platform terms and account support.

Where can I browse product links?

Explore the FashionReps Joyagoo product catalogue, then verify the original listing, exact option and availability before ordering.

Should I combine every warehouse item?

Resolve outstanding QC questions first. Compare available shipping quotes and restrictions for the intended parcel rather than assuming one large parcel is always cheaper.

What if a source link changes?

Retain the original reference and mark the row for review. Recheck seller, variant and price before treating another listing as a replacement.

Does the CSV calculate freight?

No. It records your current route quote and parcel details. Confirm charges and billable-weight rules with the service.
